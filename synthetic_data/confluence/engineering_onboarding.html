Access you need on Day 1

  • GitHub: Request access in #eng-access. Priya approves within 1 business day.
  • AWS: Raise a Jira ticket ENG-ACCESS with your role. Do not use personal AWS credentials ever.
  • LaunchDarkly (feature flags): Ask Marcus Rivera for access.
  • Snowflake (data warehouse): Request via James Okafor — he reviews all data access for compliance.
  • PagerDuty: Auto-provisioned on Day 2 by Priya's onboarding script.

How we deploy code

We use GitHub Actions for CI/CD. Every PR must pass: unit tests, integration tests, and a security scan (Snyk). Deployments to production require 2 approvals — one from your team lead and one from Priya (for infrastructure changes) or Marcus (for backend/API changes).

Why 2 approvals? After the outage in November 2023 caused by a single-approval deploy that broke the payments API, we changed the policy. Full post-mortem in ENG-POST-203.

On-call rotation

All engineers join the on-call rotation after 3 months. Schedule is in PagerDuty. Escalation path: on-call engineer → Marcus (backend) or Priya (infra) → Sarah (product decisions). Never escalate directly to James unless it's a data breach — he's flagged that as a preference.
