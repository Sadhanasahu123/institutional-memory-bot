NovaPay v2.0 - Product Launch Specification

Product Team > Roadmap 2024 > NovaPay v2.0

Overview

NovaPay v2.0 is our next-generation payment gateway that replaces the legacy Stripe-only integration with a multi-provider architecture. This document captures all decisions made between January and February 2024 by the core product team.

Key Decisions

Decision 1: Multi-provider vs Single-provider

After a 3-week debate (see Slack thread in #product-novapay and Jira ticket NOVA-112), the team decided on multi-provider. The reasoning: reducing vendor lock-in after Stripe's 2023 pricing increase. Dissenting voice: Marcus Rivera argued single-provider reduces integration complexity. Final call made by Sarah Chen on 2024-01-28.

Decision 2: Feature Flagging Strategy

All new payment providers will be behind feature flags using our internal LaunchDarkly setup. This was decided in the all-hands on 2024-02-01 (transcript in /meetings/). Ownership: DevOps team (lead: Priya Nair).

Decision 3: GDPR Compliance for Payment Data

Legal (James Okafor) reviewed and confirmed: payment tokens must be stored in EU region only. No raw card data stored anywhere. PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance required before launch. See email thread from James on 2024-01-20.
